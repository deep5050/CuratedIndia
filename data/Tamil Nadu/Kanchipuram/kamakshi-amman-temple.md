# Kamakshi Amman Temple

![Kamakshi Amman Temple](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1582510003544-4d00b7f74220)

An ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Kamakshi, one of the highest forms of Goddess Parvati.

**Note:** It is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas and a very prominent shrine in Tamil Nadu.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KamakshiAmmanTemple)

A major spiritual center that is deeply intertwined with the history of Kanchipuram.
