# Ekambareswarar Temple

<img width="480" height="360" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/809db844-962b-4c89-ac2f-3f2bfda25514" />

The Ekambareswarar Temple (also known as Ekambaranathar Temple) is the largest temple in Kanchipuram and one of the most sacred Shiva temples in India. It is one of the Pancha Bhoota Sthalams, representing the element Earth (Prithvi)

**Note:** The temple complex is massive and features a lofty gopuram and a famous 3,500-year-old mango tree.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/EkambareswararTemple)

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this is the largest temple in Kanchipuram.
