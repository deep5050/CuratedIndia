# Kailasanathar Temple

![Kailasanathar Temple](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1621840888257-2276cb04dcbb)

The oldest structure in Kanchipuram, built by the Pallava dynasty.

**Note:** Known for its historical significance and exquisite Dravidian architecture, featuring intricate sandstone carvings.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KailasanatharTemple)

A beautiful sandstone temple predominantly dedicated to Lord Shiva.
