# Varadharaja Perumal Temple

![Varadharaja Perumal Temple](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1620025916327-14283803dc4a)

A highly revered temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu.

**Note:** It is one of the Divya Desams, the 108 temples of Vishnu visited by the 12 poet saints, or Alwars.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VaradharajaPerumalTemple)

Features a stunning 100-pillared hall with intricate carvings and massive stone chains.
