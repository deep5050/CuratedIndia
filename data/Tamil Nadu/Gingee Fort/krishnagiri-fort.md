# Krishnagiri Fort

![Krishnagiri Fort](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1580211287955-af5a5cd41c2c)

A separate, self-contained fortified citadel located to the north of Rajagiri.

**Note:** Known as the English Mountain, it features intricate defensive walls and stone structures.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KrishnagiriFort)

Krishnagiri contains two stone granaries, a pillared hall, and two temples dedicated to Ranganatha. The hill offers an excellent panoramic view of the complete Gingee fort complex and its 13 kilometers of massive perimeter walls.
