# Chandrayandurg Fort

![Chandrayandurg Fort](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1549429443-4ccbeff33719)

Also known as Rani Fort, this hill is located to the southeast of the complex.

**Note:** It holds significant defensive structures and its own citadel.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ChandrayandurgFort)

Along with Rajagiri and Krishnagiri, Chandrayandurg completes the formidable defensive triangle of Gingee Fort. It witnessed the strategic maneuvers and the changing hands of power among the Vijayanagara rulers, Nayakas, Marathas, Mughals, and eventually colonial powers like the French and British.
