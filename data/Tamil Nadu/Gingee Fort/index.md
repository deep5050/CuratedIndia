# Gingee Fort


Known as the "Troy of the East", Gingee Fort is a massive, historic fortification complex.

**Note:** It is unique because it is spread across three interconnected hillocks, forming a defensive triangle.

[Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GingeeFort)

Gingee Fort has a long, turbulent history dating back to the 9th century and the Chola dynasty. It was captured by Maratha King Shivaji in 1677 and underwent an eight-year-long siege by the Mughals. Welcome to this legendary monument!
