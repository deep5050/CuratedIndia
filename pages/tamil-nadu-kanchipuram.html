The Ultimate Bucket List

Kanchipuram

Known as the City of Thousand Temples, Kanchipuram is a famous pilgrimage site in Tamil Nadu.

💡 It is also famous for its hand-woven Kanchipuram silk sarees.

Explore Locations in Kanchipuram

Ekambareswarar Temple

One of the Pancha Bhoota Stalas, representing the element Earth (Prithvi).

💡 The temple complex is massive and features a lofty gopuram and a famous 3,500-year-old mango tree.
Explore Details →

Kailasanathar Temple

The oldest structure in Kanchipuram, built by the Pallava dynasty.

💡 Known for its historical significance and exquisite Dravidian architecture, featuring intricate sandstone carvings.
Explore Details →

Kamakshi Amman Temple

An ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Kamakshi, one of the highest forms of Goddess Parvati.

💡 It is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas and a very prominent shrine in Tamil Nadu.
Explore Details →

Varadharaja Perumal Temple

A highly revered temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu.

💡 It is one of the Divya Desams, the 108 temples of Vishnu visited by the 12 poet saints, or Alwars.
