Gingee Fort

Known as the "Troy of the East", Gingee Fort is a massive, historic fortification complex.

💡 It is unique because it is spread across three interconnected hillocks, forming a defensive triangle.

Chandrayandurg Fort

Also known as Rani Fort, this hill is located to the southeast of the complex.

💡 It holds significant defensive structures and its own citadel.

Krishnagiri Fort

A separate, self-contained fortified citadel located to the north of Rajagiri.

💡 Known as the English Mountain, it features intricate defensive walls and stone structures.

Rajagiri Fort

The principal and highest hill of the Gingee Fort complex.
