Rajagiri Fort

The principal and highest hill of the Gingee Fort complex.

💡 It reaches an elevation of about 800 feet and is considered the most impregnable part of the complex.

Rajagiri serves as the main citadel. It houses the Kalyana Mahal, a multi-story square structure, and various granaries, magazines, and temples. It successfully defended the Marathas against the Mughal army's monumental eight-year siege.
